# Who Holds the Kill Switch?

## A business owner’s takeaway

Presented by Dei’Marlon Scisney, founder of The Data Guys™, for Black Owned Business Excellence. The goal is not to choose between useful AI and accountability; it is to make the conditions for responsible use concrete.

## Four questions to keep asking

- **Capability:** what can this particular system actually do, and how was that tested?
- **Control:** who can authorize, pause or stop its actions?
- **Consequences:** who benefits, and who bears the cost when it fails?
- **Accountability:** can an affected person understand and challenge a consequential decision?

## Keep the policy categories straight

- **AI Kill Switch Act, H.R. 9917:** proposes DHS/CISA emergency-order authority and technical controls for covered systems. [Introduced bill text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr9917ih/html/BILLS-119hr9917ih.htm).
- **AI Emergency Button Act, S. 5417:** the sponsor describes company-operated human shutdown mechanisms; a unanimous-consent request was blocked, not defeated in a roll-call vote. [Kennedy’s explanation](https://www.kennedy.senate.gov/2026/09/16/senate-blocks-kennedy-bill-to-require-ai-developers-to-install-an-emergency-kill-switch/).
- **Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act:** the Sanders–Casar proposal would prohibit ASI, pause advanced development pending a new regulatory structure, and create a Department of AI; the bill number was not independently verified for this briefing. [Official proposal](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/press-releases/news-sanders-casar-introduce-legislation-to-create-new-federal-agency-to-ban-artificial-superintelligence-pause-advanced-ai-development/).
- **Artificial Intelligence Risk Evaluation Act, S. 2938:** proposes a Department of Energy evaluation program with required participation for covered frontier systems. [Introduced bill text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119s2938is/pdf/BILLS-119s2938is.pdf).
- **Stop Rogue AI Act, H.R. 10362:** proposes agent-security standards and a federal procurement pathway, not a blanket private-sector ban on agents. [Introduced bill text](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/BILLS-119hr10362ih/html/BILLS-119hr10362ih.htm).

These are presented as proposals, not enacted laws, in the September 25, 2026 morning research snapshot. Status was cross-checked with indexed congressional records, GovInfo and sponsor materials; the full introduced texts for the Kennedy and Sanders–Casar proposals were not retrieved.

## Your next 30 days

These are recommended practices, not individualized legal or cybersecurity advice. Adapt them to your business and sector.

- **Week one, inventory:** list tools, connected accounts, data access, owners and shutdown controls.
- **Week two, set boundaries:** separate draft, decide and transact; set human approval requirements for high-consequence actions.
- **Week three, rehearse:** practice pausing an automation, revoking permissions, preserving logs and restoring a safe workflow.
- **Week four, review together:** measure benefits and errors, support employee training, compare vendor terms and raise community concerns.

## Ask your AI vendor

- **Data:** what do you retain, use for model training, share, delete or let me export?
- **Permissions:** can I restrict access to only the data and actions this workflow needs?
- **Approval:** can I require a person to approve payments, account changes and consequential decisions?
- **Emergency control:** can I stop an agent myself, without waiting for your support team?
- **Evidence:** can I inspect and export logs of material actions?
- **Recovery:** how are incidents reported, investigated and remediated?
- **Exit:** what happens to my data and workflows if I leave the service?

## A conversation worth continuing

Would you stop a profitable tool after one serious error? Who should pay for the safety measures that small businesses cannot afford? Would you give emergency shutdown power to a leader or company you distrust? What decision about you should always have an accessible human appeal?
