# Confidentiality & Redaction Policy

This repository shows engineering work. It does not publish client data or identify clients.

Anonymized before publication:
- Every client appears under a descriptive name, such as "Regional Sports Commission", "Community Cooperative", "Youth Technology Nonprofit" or "Multi-Company Construction Enterprise". Organization names, logos, program names, place names, email domains and staff names are replaced with fictional ones, such as "Park Partners", "Commons Park", "Summit" and "Cascade Civil".
- Vendor, project, driver and staff names in sample data are fictional.
- Personal names of client and partner staff are replaced with role titles.
- Live links to client-hosted applications are removed. The live demos on this site run on sample data.

Removed before publication:
- Client logos, letterhead, branded artwork and event materials
- Client seed workbooks, general-ledger extracts, WIP schedules and real control totals (replaced with `null` targets in `server/repro.ts`)
- Survey responses, registration exports, staff credential files and any respondent contact data
- Private source documents, vendor tickets and invoices (only labeled DEMO data remains)
- Personal email addresses and file inventories
- Compiled builds that embed client financial figures (see Case Studies)

Any remaining figures are clearly labeled demo or sample data, or come from public sources cited in the project.
