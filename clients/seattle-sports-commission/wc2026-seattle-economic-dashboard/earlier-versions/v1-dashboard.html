Evidence Classification System

Class A — Official measured data from government/agency sources (FIFA, Sound Transit, ESD, SPU)

Class B — Provider sample data from commercial platforms (CoStar/STR, Placer.ai, Square, Toast, Datafy, AirDNA, HVS)

Class C — Appropriation/authorization data from legislative sources (WA Session Law, Seattle ordinances, King County budget)

Class D — Published forecast/model, not realized outcome (Tourism Economics $929M / $845.6M projections)

Class E — Not publicly available; requires direct data-owner access (DOR tax receipts, FIFA ticket origin, granular ZIP spending)

PUBLICATION RULE
Forecasts are "verified" only in the sense that a named source really published the forecast. They are not verified realized outcomes. They must not appear in a headline that says "FIFA generated $X" until the post-event model is reconciled to actual visitor, spend, tax, labor and public-cost data.

KEY FINDINGS
Seattle saw the largest occupancy gap (-6.3pp) among all 11 host cities, despite the highest ADR growth (+30.5%). The World Cup's hotel effect came through pricing power, not occupancy. Only Los Angeles, Dallas, and San Francisco beat the baseline occupancy. Bank of America confirmed $40B global / $20B U.S. economic impact — half of FIFA's $80B projection. Placer.ai found host markets outperformed non-host by only 0.1pp in YoY retail visits.

What This Proves
Seattle hosted six sold-out World Cup matches with a verified regional footprint extending through public celebrations, downtown, the waterfront, transit, airports, ferries and team base camps. The strongest operating evidence is transportation and visitation.
WHAT THIS DOES NOT PROVE
DSA/Placer.ai visitation data is domestic only — international visitors are not captured. Visits are not deduplicated across locations. Fan Celebration "visits" may include repeats. The 3M+ downtown figure is across 6 match days, not unique visitors.

SSC Realized-Impact Methodology

1. Geography: Report Seattle, King County and Washington statewide separately

2. Event window: Match days + justified shoulder days; keep broader summer context separate

3. Incremental visitors: Combine ticket origin, Placer/Datafy, airport and lodging; deduplicate repeat visits

4. Direct visitor spend: Use visitor-origin card spend + lodging + transportation + event spend; subtract local substitution

5. Labor/indirect effects: Use transparent multipliers only after direct spend and labor income are reconciled

6. Fiscal return: Use actual DOR/City receipts, not forecasted tax rates alone

7. Displacement: Account for hotel/convention displacement, local avoidance, CID/Pioneer Square differences

8. Public cost: Actual expenditure less reimbursements/grants; disclose capital separately by useful life/legacy

9. Distribution: Report neighborhood, sector, business type, worker and statewide fan-zone outcomes

10. Confidence: Attach evidence class, data owner, extraction date and methodology to every headline metric

CORE FORMULA
Net realized benefit = incremental visitor/event direct spending + attributable labor income/indirect effects + attributable fiscal benefits − displaced normal activity − local substitution/leakage − incremental public operating costs. Capital investments should be disclosed separately with legacy/useful-life treatment.
