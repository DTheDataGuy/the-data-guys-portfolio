# Regional Sports Commission (anonymized)

## [FIFA World Cup 2026 Seattle Economic Impact Dashboard](wc2026-seattle-economic-dashboard/)

A commissioner-facing evidence product for Seattle's FIFA World Cup 2026 footprint. It covers six pillars (visitation, visitor spending, lodging, mobility, tax generation, workforce) and keeps observed data, participant reports, calculations and provider-reported estimates in separate evidence classes.

**Stack:** HTML5, JavaScript, Chart.js, CSS3, Economic modeling  
**Live demo:** [World Cup Economic Dashboard](https://dthedataguy.github.io/the-data-guys-portfolio/demos/world-cup-economic-dashboard/)

## [Follow the Dollar: Seattle Sports, Data & Policy](follow-the-dollar-teaching-story/)

An interactive, story-led teaching site for a university sports-business scholars program. It shows how multiple sources become one account of dollars reaching businesses, how funding is allocated, and how data engineering informs policy.

**Stack:** HTML5, JavaScript, Chart.js, CSS3  
**Live demo:** [Follow the Dollar](https://dthedataguy.github.io/the-data-guys-portfolio/demos/follow-the-dollar/)
