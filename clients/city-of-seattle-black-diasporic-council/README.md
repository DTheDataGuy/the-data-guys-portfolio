# City of Seattle: Black Diasporic Council Feasibility

## [Seattle Black Diasporic Community Survey Platform](community-survey-platform/)

A public survey plus a separate, restricted executive dashboard that informs whether and how to form a City of Seattle Black Diasporic Council. Responses and opt-in contact details are stored separately, and no results are exposed to unauthenticated visitors.

**Stack:** TypeScript, React 18, Vite, Express, SQLite, Drizzle ORM, Recharts, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui  
**Live:** [seattle-diaspora-survey.pplx.app](https://seattle-diaspora-survey.pplx.app)
